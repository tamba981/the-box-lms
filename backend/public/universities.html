Where we are honest

Wuteve Global Academy is not yet a degree-granting partner institution, and we do not describe ourselves as one. Here is what exists and what does not.

  • A published catalog of self-paced courses, open to anyone.
  • Verified certificates per completion, checkable by a third party.
  • No credit transfer or single sign-on for partner programmes yet; accreditation partnerships are in progress.
What we can do today

Three things that work now

📚

Supplementary material

Point students at specific courses as extra reading or catch-up material. They enrol themselves; you do not need an account to send them a link.

✓

Certificates you can check

If a student claims a completion, your office can verify it on our public page using the code on their certificate. No letter from us required.

💬

A say in what we build

We are shaping the institutional side with a small number of partners. The conversations are with the people who would use it.

ⓘ
What does not exist yet. Institutional licences, cohort provisioning for a whole faculty, bulk reporting, single sign-on and credit recognition are not built. If your question depends on any of those, we would rather tell you now than in the third meeting.

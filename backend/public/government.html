Where we are honest

We are a young institution and we are not on any government framework. What exists today is the platform; institutional arrangements are negotiated one at a time.

  • Courses anyone can review before a contract is discussed.
  • Certificates that verify publicly, by code.
  • Reporting at programme scale is not built yet.
What we can do today

Three things that work now

📚

Courses that can be reviewed first

Each course page lists its lessons and its instructor. Material can be assessed before any procurement step begins.

✓

Completions that verify

A certificate's code can be checked by anyone - an auditor, a supervisor, another agency - without contacting us.

📄

Invoiced, in writing

Engagements are quoted and invoiced in writing with agreed terms, rather than settled by card on a website.

ⓘ
What does not exist yet. Programme dashboards for ministries and agencies, bulk enrolment, single sign-on and formal accreditation are not built. If your requirement depends on those, tell us - it is exactly the kind of requirement that should shape what we build next, and we would rather say so than imply it is ready.

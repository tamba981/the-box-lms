How learning works

From choosing to finishing

Choose a course

Filter by subject, level or price. The course page shows every lesson, so you can judge whether it is the right depth before you commit.

Enrol

Free courses open straight away. For a paid course you send a mobile money or bank transfer and confirm it with our team.

Work through the lessons

Lessons include reading, video and files where the subject needs them. Your progress is kept, so stopping halfway is fine.

Finish and get the certificate

The last lesson issues your credential automatically, dated, with your name and the course title on it.

Download it, or send the code

Keep the PDF, or give an employer the verification code. They check it themselves on our public verification page.

Keep going

Completed courses stay in your dashboard with their certificates. There is no expiry, and no obligation to buy another.

Questions people ask

Before you start

Do I need to study at a set time?

No. There are no live class times and no deadlines. Lessons unlock in order, and the platform remembers where you stopped.

What does the certificate actually say?

Your name, the course title, the date you completed it, and a verification code. The credential the course offers - a certificate of completion or a diploma - is printed on it.

How does an employer check it?

They open our verification page, enter the code, and see whether the record exists. Nothing about your account is exposed - only the certificate record.

What if I pay and cannot finish?

Your enrolment does not expire, so you can come back to it. If something has gone wrong with a payment, write to info@wuteveglobalacademy.com and a person will answer.

Is there a subscription?

No. Courses are bought one at a time. The only recurring thing on this site is a mailing list you have to ask to join.

Can my employer buy seats for a group?

Yes - that is the business programme. If you are asking on behalf of your team, start there instead.

How a Certificate Works

Three steps, and nothing to pay for the certificate itself.

1. Enrol

Free or paid

Each course is priced by its instructor. Free courses enrol you immediately; paid courses go through checkout.

2. Finish every lesson

At your own pace

Your progress is saved as you complete lessons. A certificate is issued automatically once the course reaches 100%.

3. Share the link

Publicly verifiable

Every certificate carries a number and a public verification page. Anyone can open it to confirm the holder, the course and the issue date.

Certificates are issued by Wuteve Global Academy itself. They are not accredited by, or affiliated with, any university or external institution.

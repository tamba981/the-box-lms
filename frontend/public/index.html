About Wuteve Global Academy

Wuteve Global Academy makes practical, career-focused courses affordable and self-paced. Rooted in the unshakeable majesty of our highest peak and driven by the fierce law of personal execution — ‘I am because I did’ — we believe that growth belongs to those who actively climb. Each course is priced by the instructor who wrote it: some are free, and completing any of them issues a certificate you can share with an employer.

Why Wuteve

Three things we hold to

We are a small academy, so we do not claim to be everything. These are the three promises the product is actually built around.

●

Instructors have names

Every course shows who teaches it, with their role and background. If you want to know who wrote the material before you spend an evening on it, the course page tells you.

✓

Certificates can be checked

Each certificate carries a verification code. Anyone - an employer, an admissions office - can enter it and see whether it is genuine without asking us first.

↻

Work at your own pace

Lessons unlock in order and remember where you stopped. There are no scheduled class times, so a course fits around work rather than the other way round.

From first lesson to verified

How a certificate works here

Enrol and work through the lessons

Free courses open immediately. Paid courses are settled by mobile money or bank transfer, and we confirm it with you before you start.

Finish the last lesson

Completing every lesson issues the credential the course offers and records the completion date. Nothing else to apply for.

Share the code

Your certificate carries a verification code. Enter it on our verification page - or send the page to whoever asked - and the record appears.

Three ways in

Learning alone, or bringing a group

The catalog is the same either way. What changes is who arranges it and what paperwork comes with it.
